Legend Of A Bully - Full Story & Chapters

Chapter 1: Introduction For The Ethics On Isolation Of The Bullies

Once upon a time in the place of "Legend Of A Bully", there lived good bullies and bad bullies. Good bullies are kind, gentle, and make trustworthy friends. The bad bullies approached arrogantly declaring they will destroy friendships with harsh secrets and fake plans...

Chapter 2: Broken Plans Of Bad Bullies & Fake News Buffering

The bad bullies send fake news claiming the government will kill the good bullies, using fake audio recordings from President Jhay Hamps. The villagers and good bullies stand firm against the lies and accusations...

Chapter 3: The Defender Good Bullies Together With People’s Camaraderie

With the death of the 5 bad bullies and their lies exposed, the good bullies and townspeople unite in peace, proving humility defeats arrogance and many trustworthy friends conquer few bullies.

Moral Lesson

In order to defeat the bully, you need more than friends so they can trust you. Be humble so many can help you. You can never win when nobody trusts you!
